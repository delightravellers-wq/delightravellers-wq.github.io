Delight Office: Privacy Policy

Delight Travellers, Chennai, India · Last updated 12 November 2026

About Delight Office

Delight Office is a desktop app (Windows and macOS) that Delight Travellers uses internally for staff attendance, leave, work-from-home requests, tasks and office records. It is used only by Delight Travellers staff and administrators. It is not sold or offered to the public.

Delight Office has no servers of its own. Your data stays on your computer and in your own Google account (Google Drive and Google Sheets). Delight Travellers never sends it to any other company.

Google account data we access, and why

PermissionWhat the app does with it
Your name and email address
openid email profile		Signs you in and checks whether you are a staff member or an administrator. Your email is shown in the app and recorded as "updated by" on changes you make.
Google Drive files this app creates
drive.file		Creates and uses only the folders and files that Delight Office itself makes. These include your "Delight Office" folder, attendance selfies and activity screenshots (only if your administrator switched them on and you agreed), and office records and app update files for administrators. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.
Google Sheets
spreadsheets		Reads and writes your own staff sheet ("Delight Office - <your name>"), which your administrator shares with you. It holds your profile, leave, work-from-home, tasks and attendance. Administrators also use it for the "Delight Office Data" and "Delight Office Directory" sheets. The app only opens these Delight Office sheets, by their fixed IDs, and never reads or changes any other spreadsheet.

Data the app may collect at work

  • Attendance: your sign-in and sign-out times and, if your administrator enables it, a selfie taken at sign-in or sign-out. The selfie is saved in your own Google Drive.
  • Activity tracking (off by default): only if your administrator switches it on and you agree to the exact items, and only during work hours. It can record active versus idle time, the number of key presses and clicks per minute (never which keys or what you type), and periodic screenshots, which are saved in your own Google Drive.
  • Leave, work-from-home and tasks that you enter.

How data is stored and shared

  • Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on your computer. Signing out removes it.
  • Your data is shared only through normal Google Drive sharing between you and Delight Travellers administrators. Other staff cannot open your sheet.
  • We do not sell data, show ads, or use Google user data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.
  • Delight Office's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Records are kept while you work with Delight Travellers and as required by Indian labour, tax and GST law.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
  • To ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your data, email us at the address below.
